Free worksheets

The fill-in tools from the book, printable: the registrations at a glance and the pre-flight checklist, the Project Pitch worksheet with its character limits, proposal checklists for NSF and Defense, a blank Phase I budget with each line’s rule, a free-help contact sheet, the one-page “is this from the government?” test, and a log to keep from the first pitch to the final report. Free to copy. FAST organizations, SBDC and APEX Accelerator counselors, university technology-transfer offices, and any business-assistance organization may reproduce and distribute these for client use without asking.

Your state's SBIR program

Chapter 4 names five state programs that match a federal award or pay toward a proposal. Every state's program, what it pays in the state's own words, and its current window are on one page: the state directory.

The agencies change fastest

Chapter 14 and Appendix E record what each agency’s own page said when the book was written. Ceilings, cycles and portals change more often than anything else in the book. When one of them changes, it goes in the corrections below, with the date and the source.

Corrections, dated

The copyright page carries a “current as of” date. Anything found to be wrong or out of date after that is listed here with the date it was found, which printing it appears in, what the book says, what is right, and the primary source. Each correction goes into the next printing. Nothing is changed silently. Corrections from counselors and readers are credited by role, never by name.
