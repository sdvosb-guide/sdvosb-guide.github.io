State SBIR and STTR programs

What each state's own program pays, in the state's own words. Companion to Chapter 4 of The SBIR First-Timer's Handbook. Read on each state's page in September 2026; corrections welcome.

Two things to know before you plan around any of these. Most state matches are paid only after you have won the federal award, so they help with the gap between phases more than with a first proposal. And amounts, windows, and eligibility change with each state budget: open the state's page and confirm before you count on the money.

    "Applications are currently closed." / "Stay tuned for details on upcoming rounds"
    https://innovatealabama.org/programs/supplemental-grant-program/ (the organization the state designates, not a .gov address)
